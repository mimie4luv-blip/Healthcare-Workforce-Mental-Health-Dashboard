## Overview

Healthcare professionals work in demanding environments that can impact their mental wellbeing, job satisfaction, and overall workforce retention. This project explores key workforce mental health indicators, including stress levels, burnout frequency, mental health absences, Employee Assistance Program (EAP) access, and turnover intention.

Using Excel, SQL Server, and Power BI, I analyzed healthcare workforce data to uncover trends, identify major workplace challenges, and provide recommendations that support employee wellbeing and organizational performance.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* SQL Server
* Power BI

## Key Objectives

* Analyze employee stress levels across departments.
* Identify major workplace factors contributing to stress.
* Examine burnout frequency among healthcare workers.
* Assess job satisfaction levels.
* Evaluate mental health absence trends.
* Analyze workforce retention and turnover intention.

## Key Insights

* Stress levels were consistently high across several departments.
* Heavy workload emerged as the leading workplace stress factor.
* Burnout remains a significant challenge within the healthcare workforce.
* Mental health absences continue to impact employee productivity.
* A large percentage of employees indicated turnover intention.

## Recommendations

Organizations can improve workforce wellbeing by strengthening mental health support programs, addressing workload concerns, promoting work-life balance, and implementing effective employee retention strategies.
